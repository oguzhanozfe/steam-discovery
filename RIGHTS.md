# Rights and reuse

Steam Discovery combines original software and editorial work with citations,
links and public metadata about third-party games and publications. Public
access to this repository does not place any part of it in the public domain.

## Project code and editorial work

Unless a file says otherwise, the source code, site design, original research
notes and editorial text are copyright Oğuzhan Özdemir. No open-source or
content license is granted at this time. You may inspect the repository and
link to its public pages; copying, republishing or distributing substantial
parts requires permission.

The research workflow uses AI assistance for collection, drafting and
validation. Oğuzhan selects the subjects, defines the structure, reviews the
sources and is responsible for the published edits and corrections.

## Third-party material

Game names, trademarks, store artwork, publication titles and linked articles
belong to their respective rights holders. Their appearance supports
identification, criticism and research context. It does not imply sponsorship,
endorsement or a transfer of rights. Follow each original source's terms before
reusing its material.

The project-specific social image at `public/og.png` is AI-assisted artwork
created for Steam Discovery. It is covered by the project rights above and does
not contain a license to reuse third-party game art.

## Corrections and requests

For attribution corrections or rights concerns, open a GitHub issue with the
affected page, source and requested change. Do not include confidential data or
credentials.
